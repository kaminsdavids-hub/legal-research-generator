## 2. 15 C.F.R. 734.7
**Published information and software**

- status recorded: `in_force`
- check against: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-VII/subchapter-C/part-734/section-734.7
- my status note: Currently operative provision of the Export Administration Regulations.

Passages I wrote:
  1. Information is published, and so is not subject to the EAR, when it has been made available to the public without restrictions upon its further dissemination.
  2. Publication includes availability through libraries open to the public, through unlimited distribution at a conference or meeting open to the public, and through posting on the Internet where it may be accessed by any interested party.
  3. The published exclusion has historically performed constitutional-avoidance work, keeping the export control regime clear of ordinary scientific publication.

- my status note: Berman Amendment exception to IEEPA authority; currently operative.

## 5. 15 C.F.R. 734.13(b)
**Export - release of technology or source code to a foreign person in the United States**

- status recorded: `in_force`
- check against: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-VII/subchapter-C/part-734/section-734.13
- my status note: Currently operative deemed-export provision of the EAR.

Passages I wrote:
  1. Release of technology or source code subject to the EAR to a foreign person in the United States is deemed to be an export to the foreign person's most recent country of citizenship or permanent residency.
  2. A deemed export may require a license even though no item physically leaves the United States.
  3. The provision is the mechanism by which a control on model artifacts would reach foreign graduate students working in a United States university laboratory.

- status recorded: `in_force`
- check against: https://irp.fas.org/offdocs/nsdd/nsdd-189.htm
- my status note: Issued Sept. 21, 1985; repeatedly reaffirmed as standing federal policy. Executive policy, not a statute or regulation, and therefore persuasive rather than binding on a court.

Passages I wrote:
  1. It is the policy of this Administration that, to the maximum extent possible, the products of fundamental research remain unrestricted.
  2. Fundamental research means basic and applied research in science and engineering, the results of which ordinarily are published and shared broadly within the scientific community.
  3. Where national security requires control, the mechanism for control of information generated during federally funded fundamental research is classification.
